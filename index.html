How the model works

Each supplier gets eight risk signals scaled from 0 to 1: on-time delivery, defect rate, lead-time trend, inventory gap (lead time plus delay minus days of cover), transport delay, capacity utilisation, single-source dependency and financial health. A weighted logistic model turns them into a probability of disruption. Each signal’s weighted share is shown as the “why”, so planners can check the reasoning. Rules map the biggest drivers to mitigation actions. The weights are expert-set starting values; with real history they can be fitted with logistic regression or gradient boosting.
